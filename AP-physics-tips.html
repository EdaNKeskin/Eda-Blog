AP Physics Tips

  • Understand variable definitions, what object it applies to, and what not to include
    1. example: Magnetic Force on Wire 2 by 1 = current of 2 x magnetic field of 1 x portion of 2 that's in magnetic field of 1
  • While practicing, pay attention to magnitude of results. Does it sound realistic? This will help in tests
    1. example: consider the size of a small resistor, does 2000 ohms make sense?
  • READ questions CAREFULLY:
    1. “Linear/Surface/Volume density” < type matters. Mention of density gives a BIG HINT that you should set up a ratio for Q enclosed or I enclosed
    2. “Write your answer in terms of __ and physical constants”
    3. Find magnetic FORCE (not field)/ CURRENT goes out of page
  • AP Classroom videos can be a good resource; boring but good.
  • Don’t trust ChatGPT. If something is fishy, correct it and see if it changes its mind. (Or just ask a friend!)
  • You need to do more practice than is given, especially if you think:
    1. “It makes sense when we go over the problem but I can’t do it myself”
    2. “I don’t know what formulas apply here”
  • You need to study the concepts (either by asking questions or yt videos), especially if you think:
    1. “I don’t know what value to plug in”
    2. “I keep getting the same question/idea wrong”
  • General studying for tests:
    1. watch good explanation videos (short + good ENM video)
    2. ⁠watch mcq practice and solutions
    3. ⁠do prac frqs with solutions
